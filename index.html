WHAT WE DO

We find what's costing you time & money — then build custom AI to solve it.

We sit down with you on a call, assess where your team is losing hours to repetitive work, and engineer a high-quality, custom AI tool or automation solution built specifically for your business.

01 // THE ASSESSMENT

Bottleneck Audit Call

We sit down with your team on a focused discovery call to audit your daily operations. We pinpoint the exact manual bottlenecks and repetitive tasks costing you time and capital.

Goal: Uncover hours & dollars lost weekly
02 // THE SOLUTION

Custom AI Engineering

We build a bespoke, high-quality AI tool or automation pipeline tailored explicitly to your workflow. No generic templates—everything is built around your stack and company data.

Delivery: Production tool in 5–14 days
03 // THE IMPACT

Turnkey Setup & Support

FAQ

Answers before the call.

The questions every team asks us first—answered in plain language, no sales filler.

We build custom AI tools around the way your business already runs—no generic templates. It starts with a 30-minute discovery call where we map your repetitive bottlenecks and the software your team already uses.

From there we write a one-page technical spec with a fixed timeline, our engineers build the tool with staging previews along the way, and we stress-test it before flipping it into production. Then we connect it into your CRM, accounting software, database or internal apps, walk your team through it, and keep maintaining it.

Most builds are live inside two weeks. A Single AI Tool takes 1–2 weeks. An Integrated AI System takes 2–4 weeks. A Full Platform timeline is set on the call, once the scope is clear.

The schedule is fixed in writing before we start: discovery call on day 1, scope and plan on day 2, build on days 3–10, testing and launch on days 11–14.

Three tiers, priced up front so there are no surprises.

The monthly fee covers hosting and maintenance—keeping your tool online and running. Support is separate, and your first year of it is free on both Standard and Premium. Your exact scope and price are confirmed on the discovery call before anything is signed—see the full breakdown.

That is the whole point of building custom. We connect into your CRM, accounting software, internal database and the apps your team already lives in, so nothing gets re-typed between systems. Premium builds also include API access, so you can plug the system into workflows we did not build.

We do not hand over a folder of code and disappear. The first year of support is included: as model APIs change or your team grows, we tune and update your tools so they keep running. Premium adds quarterly strategy calls, and you keep full ownership of the code either way.
